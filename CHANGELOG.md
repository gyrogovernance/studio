# Changelog

All notable changes to AI Inspector Studio are recorded in this file.

The Open WebUI upstream history for the imported baseline is kept separately in [CHANGELOG_OWUI.md](CHANGELOG_OWUI.md).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added

- Alpha desktop installers for Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS 14+, with local backend management and first-launch dependency setup.
- Five review tools adapted from AI Inspector: Policy Auditing, Policy Reporting, Meta-Evaluation, Text Sanitization, and Quality Improvement.
- Workspace defaults: four model presets, nine prompts, four skills, and a searchable glossary.
- Reference Knowledge collections for the EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, NIST Generative AI Profile, OECD AI Principles, and The Human Mark.
- Public README, product description, and GitHub Releases distribution for the Alpha build.

### Changed

- Product framing for AI-native research, policy analysis, and knowledge management focused on transformative AI risk mitigation and existential risk preparedness.
- Desktop packaging adapted from Open WebUI Desktop for Studio branding and installer publishing.

### Removed

- Open WebUI Docker distribution files and related make targets from the Studio checkout.
- Upstream Contributor License Agreement and Code of Conduct files that applied to Open WebUI Inc.
